History

Founded with a deep love for rushing rivers and outdoor exploration, our company has grown from a small local group of river enthusiasts into a premier destination for whitewater adventurers. Over the years, we have safely guided thousands of guests through breathtaking rapids, creating lifelong memories while respecting and preserving our natural river ecosystems.
